Four stations, one record

A visitor moves through each station in turn. Each officer sees the same record, and can act only on their own step.

  1. STATION 1 · COMPUTER 1

    Gate reception

    Access Control Building

    Confirm the visitor is approved, verify them against the UNDSS watchlist, and admit. Register walk-ins.

  2. STATION 2 · COMPUTER 2

    Documentation & tags

    Main Access Control Building

    Record the ID document checked, place ID in physical safe custody, and issue a numbered visitor tag.

  3. STATION 3 · COMPUTER 3

    Internal reception

    Main UN House Building

    Receive documented visitors and VIP guests, contact the host to receive them, check them in, and collect tags on departure.

  4. STATION 4 · TABLET

    VIP gate

    VIP Diplomatic Canopy

    Verify VIP guests against the approved list and send them to Internal Reception (Station 3), where the host is contacted.

Access Control Architecture

Floor Restrictions & Colour-Coded Identification

UN House combines pre-registration, security-controlled floor access, explicit destination-only clearance, and visual lanyard identification. Access to unauthorized floors or unescorted movement across agency wings is strictly prohibited.

UN House Standard · 5-Tier System
BLUE · General Visitor

Host-authorized meetings, interviews, conferences. Access limited to approved floor & office.

GREEN · Registry & Mail

Official correspondence, diplomatic pouch & parcel dispatch. Controlled access to ground floor Registry only.

AMBER · Contractor

Maintenance, facilities, IT vendors. Work-order restricted with mandatory escort and building bounds.

PURPLE · Official Protocol

Official diplomatic engagements and government delegations handled through designated protocol.

⭐ VIP · Lanyard-Exempt

Ambassadors, Ministers & Heads of Mission received at VIP canopy. Exempt from physical lanyards under protocol.

What it handles

Built to the UN Common Services requirements for visitor, contractor and VIP access across 16 resident agencies.

Registration

Single visitors, groups and walk-ins, bulk upload by CSV or photo scan, and advance registration by staff hosts.

Approvals

Every visitor, VIP and contractor request goes to an approver before becoming expected at the gate. No one can approve their own request.

🏷️

Tags and badges

Unique reference numbers, tag assignment, digital phone passes, and physical safe tracking of every tag issued and returned.

Contractors

Access limited by building and date, expiry warnings, multi-day re-entry, and immediate suspension when needed.

Live dashboard

Expected visitors, who is on site, occupancy by building and agency, overstays and security exceptions.

Reports and history

Searchable visitor history, daily to monthly counts, evacuation roll calls, and CSV audit reports for management.

Security and accountability

Access rules are enforced on the server for every read and write, not just hidden in the screen.

UNDSS Physical Security Standard Level 2
Aligned with Nigeria NDPA 2023
UN HLCM Data Privacy Framework
UN Security Council Res 1267 Active
  • Role-based access (RBAC) Gate, documentation, reception, VIP, host, approver and administrator roles, each strictly partitioned to its operational post.
  • Server-Enforced Audit Trail (/audit) Every registration, approval, check-in and check-out is committed to an append-only server log (/audit). Client browsers have zero permissions to delete or alter historical entries.
  • Data Minimisation & Physical ID Custody Zero physical ID scans, photocopies, or biometrics are uploaded or stored. Original IDs are held in physical security custody at Station 2 and returned upon exit.
  • Staff accounts & Governance Quorum Each staff member signs in with their UN identity. Administrator roles are strictly capped at 3 with mutual peer audit oversight.
