Simple 4-Station Process

How It Works

From perimeter arrival to compound departure, every step is managed with transparency, security, and institutional accountability.

Station 1

Gate Reception

Confirm pre-approval, verify against the live UNDSS watchlist, and admit cleared visitors.

Station 2

Documentation & Tags

Inspect physical Government photo ID in person and issue a numbered colour-coded tag.

Station 3

Internal Reception

Receive documented guests, alert host officer, complete check-in, and collect tags on departure.

Station 4

VIP Diplomatic Gate

Ambassadors and Ministers fast-tracked with diplomatic courtesy and protocol officer escort.
