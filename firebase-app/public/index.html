Questions

Good to know.

How do I get an invitation?
Ask the UN staff member you're meeting. They create a secure link for you from the system, and you fill in your own details. Each link works once and expires after 72 hours.
I didn't get a link. Can I just walk in?
Unscheduled visits may be refused or delayed. Gate staff will call your host to confirm before you're admitted, so pre-registering is always faster.
What ID should I bring?
A valid government photo ID: National ID (NIN), international passport, driver's licence or diplomatic ID. It's checked in person at the gate and you get it back when you leave.
What happens to my personal information?
Your details are used only to manage your visit and site security. ID documents are inspected in person and not scanned or uploaded.
I'm a contractor. How do I get access?
Your UN focal point requests dated site access for you through the system. Once the Facilities Security Manager clears it, you're expected at the gate for those days.
I'm UN staff. How do I sign in?
Use Staff sign-in with your UN agency email address. We email you a one-time sign-in link, so there's no password to remember.
