Four stations, one record

A visitor moves through each station in turn. Each officer sees the same record, and can act only on their own step.

  1. STATION 1 · COMPUTER 1

    Gate reception

    Access Control Building

    Confirm the visitor is approved, verify them and admit. Register walk-ins.

  2. STATION 2 · COMPUTER 2

    Documentation & tags

    Main Access Control Building

    Record the ID document checked and issue a numbered visitor tag.

  3. STATION 3 · COMPUTER 3

    Internal reception

    Main UN House Building

    Receive documented visitors and VIP guests, contact the host to receive them, check them in, and collect tags on departure.

  4. STATION 4 · TABLET

    VIP gate

    VIP Gate

    Verify VIP guests against the approved list and send them to Internal Reception (Station 3), where the host is contacted.

What it handles

Built to the UN Common Services requirements for visitor, contractor and VIP access.

Registration

Single visitors, groups and walk-ins, bulk upload by CSV or scan, and advance registration by staff hosts.

Approvals

Every visitor, VIP and contractor request goes to an approver before becoming expected at the gate. No one can approve their own request.

Tags and badges

Unique reference numbers, tag assignment, digital phone passes, and tracking of every tag issued and returned.

Contractors

Access limited by building and date, expiry warnings, multi-day re-entry, and immediate suspension when needed.

Live dashboard

Expected visitors, who is on site, occupancy by building and agency, overstays and exceptions.

Reports and history

Searchable visitor history, daily to monthly counts, evacuation roll calls, and CSV reports for management.

Security and accountability

Access rules are enforced on the server for every read and write, not just hidden in the screen.

  • Role-based access. Gate, documentation, reception, VIP, host, approver and administrator roles, each strictly partitioned to its operational post.
  • Server-Enforced Audit Trail. Every registration, approval, check-in and check-out is committed to an append-only server log (/audit). Client browsers have zero permissions to delete or alter historical entries.
  • Data Minimisation & Physical ID Custody. Zero physical ID scans, photocopies, or biometrics are uploaded or stored. Original IDs are held in physical security custody at Station 2 and returned upon exit.
  • Staff accounts & Governance. Each staff member signs in with their UN identity. Administrator roles are strictly capped at 3 with peer audit oversight.
