Operational Architecture · Four Stations, One Record

A Seamless, Sequential Physical Intake Pipeline

A visitor moves through each physical station in turn. Every officer sees the exact same real-time perimeter record, with server-enforced permissions strictly partitioned to their assigned post.

STATION 1 · STEP 01

Gate Reception

Access Control Building (Computer 1)

Confirm visitor advance approval, verify against the live UNDSS watchlist, and admit cleared guests through the perimeter turnstiles. Register unscheduled walk-ins.

STATION 2 · STEP 02

Documentation & Tags

Main Access Control Desk (Computer 2)

Verify Government photo ID, place physical ID into secure custody slot, log declared equipment, and issue a numbered colour-coded physical lanyard tag.

STATION 3 · STEP 03

Internal Reception

Main UN House Building (Computer 3)

Receive documented visitors, contact host on desk phone (Ext. 1000) or WhatsApp, check them in upon host arrival, and collect physical tags on exit.

STATION 4 · VIP PROTOCOL

VIP Diplomatic Gate

VIP Diplomatic Canopy (Tablet 1)

Verify accredited Ambassadorial delegations, Ministers, and Heads of Mission against expected lists. Fast-track with direct Protocol Officer alert (Lanyard-Exempt).

Floor Restrictions & 5-Tier Colour-Coded System

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Server-Partitioned
    Gate, documentation, reception, VIP, host, approver, and administrator roles. Each terminal strictly restricted to its physical operational post.
  • Server-Enforced Audit Trail (/audit) Append-Only
    Every registration, clearance, physical tag handover, check-in, and check-out is committed to an immutable server log. Client browsers possess zero deletion privileges.
  • Data Minimisation & Physical Safe Custody Zero Cloud IDs
    Zero physical ID scans, photocopies, or biometrics uploaded or stored. Original physical Government IDs are held in physical security safe custody and returned upon exit.
  • Staff Governance & Quorum Quorum of 3
    Staff sign in with their authenticated UN email. System Administrator roles are capped at 3 with mutual peer oversight and automated access review logs.
