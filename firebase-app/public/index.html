Operational Integrity

The Four-Station Security Architecture

Access control is physically partitioned across four distinct operational posts. No single station can bypass or duplicate the checks of another.

STATION 1
🛡️

Gate Reception

Outer Perimeter Gate

Perimeter guards screen expected guests, verify UNDSS clearance, allocate next available physical tag from the active tray rack, and admit visitors into the compound.

• Admitted at Station 1
STATION 2
🪪

Documentation

Access Control Building

UNDSS Standard Operating Procedures

Enterprise Security & Compound Governance

Built strictly to the United Nations Security Management System (UNSMS) guidelines and Nigeria National Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023).

🚨

3-Tier Watchlist Screening

Every clearance and gate admission runs real-time matching against UNDSS Local Security Bans, UN Security Council (UNSC ISIL/Al-Qaida 1267), and Nigerian National Security stoplists (ONSA/NCTC).

🔒

Zero Biometric & ID Scans Stored

Completely eliminates identity theft and privacy risk. Zero national ID scans, photocopies, or biometrics are ever uploaded to cloud servers. Physical IDs are securely held in custody at Station 2.

📱

Anti-Screenshot Dynamic Pass

Approved visitors receive an encrypted digital pass featuring a synchronized real-time millisecond pulsing clock and dynamic QR token, completely preventing counterfeit screenshot fraud.

🛠️

Contractor & Equipment Custody

Multi-day maintenance contractors are restricted by building, room, and permit expiry. Specialized tools and equipment passes are logged with serial numbers and gate-exit verification.

📜

Immutable Server Audit Trail

Every registration, FSM decision, gate admittance, tag exchange, and departure is written directly to an append-only server audit log (/audit). Cannot be altered or deleted by client browsers.

🚨

Evacuation & Roll-Call Ready

One-click real-time muster roll call by building and agency. Immediate accounting of every visitor, contractor, and vehicle physically inside the compound in emergencies.
